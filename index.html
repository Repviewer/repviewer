// 01. Desktop App
Select a local folder in the desktop app to load your recipe files. It automatically formats everything and syncs it to your server so you never copy files by hand again.
// 02. Web App
Need your recipes on your phone or a second screen? The web app is built for devices where you can't run the desktop client. Sign in once, stay signed in, and your whole cookbook opens instantly — even mid-cooking, with your screen staying awake while a recipe is open.
// 03. Family Sharing
Share your family recipe collection seamlessly with everyone at home. Invite family and friends, set who can view or edit, and let everyone cook from the exact same up-to-date cookbook — no more "which version is the real one?"
